Our story

Proof that respects the people in it.

Mannatech South Africa's associates have always had the same tool for sharing results: a screenshot and a hope. Mtech Testimonials replaces that with a proper home for real stories: submitted, reviewed, categorised, and shared with the care that health claims demand.

It is two products in one. A public site where associates and their prospects browse, filter, download and share testimonials. And an admin workspace where every submission is reviewed against Mannatech's "Golden Rule" before it goes live: with AI helping catch duplicates and imports flowing in from CSV, Telegram and WhatsApp. The brand's job is to make all of it feel credible and warm, never clinical and never salesy.

Logo

The mark

The logo pairs a teal-to-lime leaf (growth, wellness, nature) with a calm humanist wordmark and a "Testimonials Database" pill. Always place it as a supplied file; never redraw it, and never paste its SVG paths into a page.

Clear space & minimum size

Keep clear space on all sides equal to the height of the leaf mark. Minimum: 140px wide for the full lockup on screen; the leaf mark alone stays legible down to 24px (favicon / avatar).

Misuse

✕Never place the colour master on a busy or unapproved background.
✕Never reduce opacity or screen the mark back.
✕Never rotate, stretch or distort the proportions.
✕Never add shadows, glows or outlines.

Colour

Teal leads, lime accents, navy anchors

Teal is the trust colour and does the heavy lifting. Lime is energy, used for fills and accents only, never body text. Navy gives weight to dark surfaces. Grounds stay light and airy so the whole thing reads clean and credible.

Accessibility note: Lime #ACC42A on white is ~2.0:1, well below readable. It is a fill colour. Teal #03B09F cannot carry white text (2.7:1); interactive teal is #027268. Both decisions are deliberate and preserve the brand hues where they pass.

Typography

One voice: Plus Jakarta Sans

The web app and the PDF export now share a single typeface: Plus Jakarta Sans. It replaces the old split (Avenir LT Pro on web, Plus Jakarta Sans in PDF), so a testimonial reads identically on screen and in the downloadable A4. Hierarchy comes from weight and size, not a second font.

Why the change: Avenir LT Pro needs Adobe Typekit at runtime and isn't available to the react-pdf renderer, which is exactly why the PDF already used Jakarta. Standardising on the font that works in both places removes the Typekit dependency and the visual mismatch in one move. Fallback: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', 'Segoe UI', 'Avenir Next', system-ui, sans-serif.

Tone of voice

Credible, warm, clean, careful

Credible

Let the stories carry the weight. Plain language, specific details, named people. No hype, no exclamation marks stacked three deep.

Warm

These are real people sharing something personal. Speak like a trusted friend who's been there, human, never corporate boilerplate.

Clean

Clear over clever. Short sentences. One idea at a time. The reader should never have to re-read to understand.

Careful

The Golden Rule is non-negotiable: describe experiences, never promise outcomes or imply a cure. "Helped me feel", not "cures".

Write this

"Since starting, Maria says her energy through the afternoon has improved, she shares her routine below."

Not this

"This PRODUCT CURED Maria's fatigue in WEEKS!! You have to try it 🔥🔥"

Write this

"Browse real testimonials from Mannatech SA associates. Every story is reviewed before it's published."

Not this

"Thousands of miracle transformations, guaranteed results or your money back!"
